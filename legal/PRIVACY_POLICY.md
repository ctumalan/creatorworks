## 1. What this policy covers

CreatorWorks helps people discover projects, share their own work, and exchange feedback. This policy explains how the operator identified above collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you use the CreatorWorks website and platform features.

Independent projects linked from CreatorWorks and third-party video services have their own privacy practices. This policy does not govern information you give directly to those services. CreatorWorks-operated projects may provide additional privacy information specific to their features.

**Your choices matter:** you can browse public listings without an account, control whether your profile is public, keep project drafts private, choose feedback visibility, and decline optional analytics. A private profile does not make a published project anonymous: its creator attribution may still show your public name, chosen photo or avatar, and identity label.

## 3. Public information, drafts, and conversations

**Public listings and profiles.** Published projects and the identity attributed to them can be viewed by other visitors. If you make your profile public, its public details are also visible. Public information can be copied or indexed by others; we cannot control independent copies after disclosure.

**Drafts.** Before a draft is saved to your account, the browser may store it on your device. After you sign in and continue the listing flow, the Service may attach that draft to your account so you can find it in My projects. An account draft is available to you and to authorized personnel or providers who need access for operation, support, safety, or legal purposes. It is not displayed in the public catalog simply because you saved it.

**Feedback and replies.** Feedback marked for public sharing may be shown after review. Private feedback and replies are available to the relevant participants. Authorized personnel may access them when necessary for operation, safety, support, or legal obligations. These conversations are not end-to-end encrypted.

**Recommendations.** Related-project suggestions may use the project you are viewing, project categories and descriptions, and your saved projects or category interests. These suggestions help you browse; they are not decisions about employment, credit, insurance, or other legal entitlements.

- Respond to support and privacy requests and send necessary service communications.
- Diagnose problems and, when you permit optional analytics, understand visits and engagement.
- Meet legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of users, creators, the company, and others.

CreatorWorks does not use your content to train general-purpose AI models. We do not treat agreement to the Terms as consent to optional analytics or marketing. If we introduce a materially different use of personal information, we will provide the required notice and obtain consent where applicable.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, the relevant legal bases are performance of our agreement for core features; legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service where not overridden by your rights; legal obligations; and consent for optional analytics or other activities requiring it. You may ask for more information about the basis for a particular use.

Our proposed Google Analytics integration disables advertising personalization and Google signals; it does not enable age or gender profiling. Any future change to those practices requires an updated notice and any legally required choices before it begins.

## 6. Cookies, device storage, and optional analytics

**Necessary features.** We use cookies and similar mechanisms for sign-in sessions, authentication security, and essential preferences. Your browser may also store drafts, saved-project selections, category interests, and analytics choices. Clearing browser storage can remove a draft that has not been saved to your account. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in or other account features.

**Optional Google Analytics.** When enabled for the Service, Google Analytics loads only after you choose to allow it. It may use cookies or similar identifiers to distinguish visits and measure engagement. We configure analytics to exclude names, email addresses, private drafts, feedback text, and search terms from the information the application deliberately sends. We do not enable advertising personalization or Google signals.

You may decline without losing access to the core Service. You can change your choice through Analytics preferences on the catalog. Withdrawal stops future optional analytics from that browser; it does not automatically erase information already lawfully collected or change choices on another device. You can separately request deletion where available under applicable law.

Our current implementation does not respond separately to legacy browser “Do Not Track” settings. Use Analytics preferences to control optional measurement. Where applicable law requires recognition of a universal opt-out signal such as Global Privacy Control, we will give it the required effect. We do not currently conduct the sale or cross-context advertising activities to which such opt-outs commonly apply.

Third parties you visit or load, including video services, may collect activity across websites under their own policies. Read [Google’s explanation of information received from sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for additional context.

## 7. External links, previews, and embedded videos

Clicking a project link takes you to its operator’s service. That operator may collect information directly from your browser. Review its privacy policy before providing information or connecting accounts.

When you request a website preview, CreatorWorks accesses the submitted public URL to create a screenshot. The destination server may receive a request from our capture service. We do not use your private browser session to sign in to that destination. Review the resulting screenshot so you do not publish information you intended to keep private.

Video players from supported services, such as YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom, are loaded when you choose to play the video. The provider may then receive your IP address, device information, and playback activity, and may use its own storage or cookies. Your analytics preference on CreatorWorks does not automatically control the video provider’s separate processing.
