# CreatorWorks legal publication notes

Prepared September 6, 2026. These are tailored drafts for the proposed CreatorWorks release, not a claim of legal compliance or a substitute for advice from licensed counsel. Neither draft has been published or made effective.

## Details to complete

- The operator’s actual legal name and business mailing address. “CreatorWorks” may be a brand; do not invent an LLC or corporation.
- Working support, privacy, and copyright contact addresses. One monitored mailbox can serve several purposes if appropriate. Do not publish an owner’s personal address without their decision to use it publicly.
- Effective date, governing jurisdiction, and court venue, with local consumer rights preserved.
- A designated copyright agent’s real contact information. If relying on applicable U.S. DMCA safe harbors, register and maintain the agent with the Copyright Office and implement the required procedures; merely posting Terms does not establish eligibility.

## Product and operational alignment

- Drafting assumes adult-only account creation. Add an accurate age representation to registration and review the policy if allowing minors.
- Use an initially unchecked checkbox for Terms acceptance. Suggested copy: “I am at least 18 years old and agree to the Terms of Service. I acknowledge the Privacy Policy.” Adapt if local adulthood is higher.
- Link both documents before registration and record the accepted Terms version, account identifier, and timestamp. A preselected checkbox or an inaccessible policy link should not stand in for meaningful assent.
- Analytics needs its own optional choice; do not bundle consent into the account checkbox. Marketing consent also remains separate.
- Verify that analytics remains off before consent, advertising features stay disabled, and event metadata cannot expose draft contents, search terms, or other personal data. Test withdrawal and any legally required preference signals.
- Confirm supplier agreements, international transfer mechanisms, retention settings, backup expiration, and access controls. Do not publish a promise of a specific certification or deadline that has not been verified.
- Account deletion in the current implementation is an administrator-reviewed request. The drafts disclose this. Staff the process, meet applicable legal deadlines, and define treatment of projects, uploads, feedback, and backups. Do not represent the button as instant erasure.
- Privacy choices need to match actual attribution: a private profile can still have a public creator name/photo on published projects.
- Staff the listed privacy and copyright inboxes, moderation appeals, incident response, and rights requests before making the documents effective.
- The license excludes training general-purpose AI models. Review the drafts before introducing AI vendors that use submitted content for training.
- Have counsel assess the liability cap, business indemnity, applicable jurisdictions, privacy-law applicability, and copyright safe-harbor procedure against the operator’s actual business.

## Research sources

- [U.S. Copyright Office — Section 512 resources](https://www.copyright.gov/512/): agent designation, notice and counter-notice procedure, repeat infringement, and safe-harbor conditions.
- [U.S. Code — 17 U.S.C. § 512](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title17-section512%28c%29%281%29): statutory notice and restoration requirements.
- [California Attorney General — CCPA](https://www.oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa): conditional applicability, notices, and consumer rights. California residency alone does not mean every business is covered by the CCPA.
- [FTC — COPPA compliance guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business): children’s data requirements; an age clause alone does not resolve child-directed-service obligations.
- [European Commission — data-protection obligations](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en): transparency, lawful bases, and rights where applicable.
- [Google Analytics — safeguarding data](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en): disclosures and consent for analytics.
