# CreatorWorks Phase 0: Next Milestone

## Status

**Approved direction: focus on CreatorWorks and continue manual validation.**

Codex Studio has completed its role as the first real validation model. The next work belongs in CreatorWorks. This milestone does not authorize additional Codex Studio development.

## What the First Session Proved

The first session provided evidence that the central CreatorWorks loop can create value:

> **Present → Share → Experience → Respond → Recognize**

- **Present:** A creator could explain real software in ordinary language.
- **Share:** A personal invitation gave the participant a reason to try it.
- **Experience:** The participant reached and used the work without the creator being physically present.
- **Respond:** The participant returned and offered specific feedback.
- **Recognize:** The feedback clearly mattered to the creator, although the acknowledgment and outcome-sharing steps still need to be completed and tested as part of CreatorWorks.

The session also exposed an important operational problem: preparing outside access required too much creator/webmaster work. CreatorWorks must eventually make a trustworthy handoff possible without requiring a creator to manage authentication systems manually.

## What Has Not Been Proved

One completed session does not yet prove that:

- Different creators can describe and share their work without extensive help.
- Different participants can understand the invitation and return path.
- The readiness experience reduces publishing anxiety responsibly.
- A CreatorWorks project page can replace repeated personal explanation.
- Acknowledgment and outcome-sharing make participation feel meaningfully reciprocal.
- The loop works across several kinds of independently created software.
- The experience can operate repeatedly without intensive facilitation.

## Smallest Next CreatorWorks Milestone

Create a **clickable, non-production CreatorWorks experience prototype** for one complete private invitation. It should let people react to the CreatorWorks experience itself without adding accounts, integrations, payments, hosting, or public discovery.

The prototype should contain only these connected moments:

1. **Welcome** — Explain CreatorWorks in warm, ordinary language and invite a creator to begin.
2. **Ready to share?** — Help the creator decide whether the work is ready for one honest conversation, not a finished public launch.
3. **Your work** — Help the creator explain what it is, who it may help, its current state, and what can actually be tried.
4. **Invite someone** — Generate a personal invitation that explains why this particular person may care.
5. **Before you try it** — Show the participant who made the work, why they were invited, what will happen next, important access or privacy limits, and the one thing the creator wants them to notice.
6. **Try the work** — Provide one clear external action and keep an obvious route back.
7. **Share what happened** — Ask the creator's one meaningful question, followed by a small amount of optional context.
8. **Thank and acknowledge** — Let the creator respond personally and recognize the participant's contribution.
9. **What changed** — Show the participant what the creator learned, decided, or improved because they participated.

These are human-facing names, not developer tabs. The flow should feel like one guided conversation rather than an administration dashboard.

## First Prototype Standard

The prototype succeeds when a creator and participant can both explain, in their own words:

- What CreatorWorks does.
- Why the work is being shared now.
- What the participant can try.
- What the creator wants to learn.
- How the participant returns after trying it.
- How the participant will know their contribution mattered.

The prototype should make the relationship clear even when the creator is not present.

## Next Validation Round

Use the prototype with a different real creator-work-participant combination when possible. Music Transcriber can become a later candidate once another person can safely reach and experience it through a stable link. Do not delay CreatorWorks prototype testing solely to wait for Music Transcriber.

For the next round, record:

- Whether the creator understood the difference between one honest invitation and public publishing.
- Whether they could prepare the project story and invitation without marketing knowledge.
- Whether the participant understood the work and why they were invited.
- Whether the trust summary prepared them honestly.
- Whether they reached the external work without live guidance.
- Whether they returned without getting lost.
- Whether the response answered the creator's chosen question.
- Whether acknowledgment and the outcome update made the participant feel recognized.
- How much manual intervention was required from the facilitator or webmaster.

## Explicit Boundaries

Do not add during this milestone:

- Creator or participant accounts.
- Automated application installation.
- The Connection Kit.
- Software hosting.
- Public discovery.
- Marketplace features.
- Payments, donations, balances, bank accounts, or withdrawals.
- Likes, followers, rankings, or social feeds.
- Automated activity tracking inside a creator's application.
- Additional Codex Studio product revisions.

Optional financial support may remain visible only as a nonfunctional concept shown after value and feedback. No money or payment information should be collected.

## Decision After This Milestone

Continue Phase 0 until the learning threshold in [PHASE_0_VALIDATION.md](PHASE_0_VALIDATION.md) is met. Move to the private founding beta only when the complete loop works across multiple creators and works without intensive explanation and without unresolved trust or safety problems.
